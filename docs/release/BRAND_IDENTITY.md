### 4.1 TetraSol logo and loader (supplied 2026-10-28)

- **Files.** Source files in `store/assets/official/tetrasol/` (with a README). `scripts/brand/prepare-supplied-assets.py` derives the app files: backdrop removed, a tagline-free **wordmark** for small sizes (the "Delaware-based incorporation, USA" line is unreadable below ~150 px of logo height) and the swirl scaled to 288 px. The artwork is never redrawn or recoloured.
- **Where the logo appears** (`TetraSolLogo` in `apps/mobile/src/ui/brand` and `apps/admin/src/components/brand.tsx`): store banner (full logo), product tiles and product pages without a photo (on the neutral tile), product pages with a photo (above the name), the home card that opens the store, approved product information under assistant answers, the admin catalogue (header, brand column, product page, brands list) and the TetraSol knowledge page. Never in the Dress My Wound lockup, the app icon or the store keywords. Products of other company brands show the brand name as text until their logo is supplied.
- **Dark surfaces.** The navy ink would disappear on dark backgrounds, so the logo sits on a light plate there (token `lightColors.surface` / `--color-logo-plate`) instead of being recoloured.
- **Loader.** `BrandLoader` (mobile: Reanimated rotation, 1.4 s per turn; admin: CSS) replaces screen-level loading (`LoadingScreen`), the app start screen, the assistant's "thinking" row, document reading, health-summary and clinical-draft generation, and admin loading states. With Reduce Motion on, it stays still and breathes. Small in-button spinners stay native.
- **Pending:** TetraSol brand-owner confirmation of these usages and of the derived wordmark crop (`NEEDS_OFFICIAL_ASSETS`, usage guidelines).

### 4.2 Dress My Wound identity (supplied 2026-10-28)

- **Received.** Two images attached by the project owner: the brand usage board (glass icon, light/dark/brand-colour/mono variants, palette) and the full wordmark with the console subtitle. Stored unchanged in `store/assets/official/dmw/reference/` with their SHA-256 (README there). **`DMW_Brand_Handoff.zip` (named in the request) was not received** — no original vector, font or icon file exists in the repository.
- **Vectorised, not redrawn.** `scripts/brand/trace-dmw-reference.py` (`pnpm brand:trace`) separates the board colours (orange, medical silver, navy) at 4× supersampling and traces each with potrace into `packages/tokens/src/dmw-artwork.ts`. Proportions, gaps and letterforms are those of the reference. Limit: the source is a ~1.7k px raster, so very fine detail is as precise as the image allows; replace the paths with the original vectors when they arrive.
- **One painter for every surface.** `packages/tokens/src/dmw.ts` applies the board colours (Primary Orange `#F97316`, Text/Dark `#1F2937`, Medical Silver `#9CA3AF`) with the reference's shading, and emits the SVG used by the asset generator, the app (`react-native-svg` `SvgXml`) and the admin console (static SVG files).
- **Two distinct assets:**
  - *External app icon* — the emblem only (cross, heart, ECG, leaf), **no text**, on a soft glass surface. Centred on its bounding box and scaled uniformly (never stretched or cropped); see §11 for every file. Android layers keep the emblem inside a 27 dp radius (66 dp safe zone = 33 dp), checked by `scripts/release/check.ts`.
  - *Internal wordmark* — emblem + "Dress My Wound", tagline-free: welcome (`Lockup`), sign in / sign up (`Header brand`), consent (`Lockup compact`), account footer ("about": wordmark + version), iOS splash. On dark surfaces "Dress My" turns soft white `#F8FAFC`; "Wound" stays orange.
  - *Console subtitle* — "Operations & clinical review console" is **live, localised text** under the wordmark in the admin console only (sidebar, sign-in), uppercase with flanking rules in English; never on patient-facing screens and never inside the artwork.
- **Arabic.** No Arabic is added to the artwork. غير لي is set as text under the wordmark (`Lockup`, larger when the app is in Arabic); the artwork keeps its left-to-right reading in RTL layouts (`direction: 'ltr'` on the mark only).

- **Placeholder removed.** The arch + care-cross placeholder mark, `apps/admin/public/mark.png` and `store/assets/shared/mark.svg` are gone; `geometry.ts` remains for the welcome hero ornament.

## 6. Orange (accent) usage rule

**Rule.** Orange is reserved for **the one hero call-to-action on a screen, selections, progress and the active-tab indicator**. It is **never** used for clinical warnings: those use the separate amber and red scale (§5.3). The rule appears in comments (`Button.tsx`, `Chip.tsx`, `TabBar.tsx`, `packages/tokens/src/index.ts`). No lint rule enforces it. The only automated guard is the token test that keeps accent distinct from warning and danger.

| Use | Where (config inspection) | Within the rule? |
|---|---|---|
| Hero CTA (`Button variant="accent"`) | `welcome.tsx` "Create an account"; `consent.tsx` accept button | Yes: one per screen |
| Selection | `Chip` (selected), `Checkbox`, radio cards (`clinician/(tabs)/availability.tsx`, `patient/product/[slug].tsx`), `Switch` track colour (privacy, availability, services, family-add) | Yes |
| Progress | Onboarding step bar (`onboarding/patient.tsx`) | Yes |
| Active indicator | Tab bar indicator (`features/TabBar.tsx`); `Segmented` indicator | Yes. The tab icon tint stays navy. |
| Focus ring | `focusRing` = orange | Accessibility affordance |
| Current-plan border | `settings/subscription.tsx` | Selection-like |
| Brand highlight (soft) | `Card tone="accent"` and `Badge tone="accent"` on the patient-home assistant card; `Badge tone="accent"` for the `CAREGIVER` family access level | Not a CTA or a clinical meaning. The brand owner should confirm it. |
| **Open:** several orange "Subscribe" buttons | `settings/subscription.tsx` renders an accent button for **each** purchasable offer, so once store subscriptions are on there could be up to one per plan × period. Today none render: `billing.store_subscriptions` is off and there are no product IDs. | **No.** It breaks "one hero CTA". Fix before enabling purchases (open, not `CODE_READY`). |

## 7. Alia Group architectural language: EXTREMELY subtle

Tokens are in `packages/tokens/src/index.ts` (`ornament`). Components are in `apps/mobile/src/ui/brand/Ornaments.tsx`.

| Element | Token / value | Where it is used (config inspection) |
|---|---|---|
| Mashrabiya / khatam lattice, hero | `latticeOpacityHero: 0.05` (5%) | **Welcome hero only** (`app/welcome.tsx`); the Play feature graphic |
| Lattice, light canvas | `latticeOpacityCanvas: 0.03` (3%) | Token and `Mashrabiya` default only. **No screen uses it today.** |
| Pointed-arch hairline | `archStrokeOpacity: 0.35` (35%), 1 px, brass/ornament colour | Welcome hero frame around the `Lockup`; the feature graphic |
| Arch fill | `rgba(247,243,236,0.03)` | Welcome hero; the feature graphic |
| Brass rule (hairline with a lozenge) | 1 px | `Lockup` only, between the name and the endorsement |
| Geometry | `archCrownRatio 0.42`, `latticeCell 28` | `geometry.ts`, shared with the asset generator |

Rules:
- Ornament **never sits behind clinical content, forms, lists or data**. Only `welcome.tsx` uses `Mashrabiya` and `ArchShape`. Config inspection by grep; no test enforces it.
- Ornaments are decorative. They are hidden from screen readers (`accessibilityElementsHidden`, `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, `aria-hidden`) and ignore touches (`pointerEvents="none"`).
- **The app icon has no texture.**
- Alia Group has not approved this interpretation of its architectural language: `NEEDS_OFFICIAL_ASSETS`.
