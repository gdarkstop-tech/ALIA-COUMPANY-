## 8. In-App Purchase (summary; details in `docs/release/PAYMENT_COMPLIANCE.md`)

Rules, all **[verified-official]** at https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ unless another URL is given:
- 3.1.1: unlocking digital features or subscriptions requires IAP, with a restore mechanism.
- 3.1.3(e): physical goods and services consumed outside the app must *not* use IAP.
- 3.1.3(d): real-time 1:1 consultations may use non-IAP payment.
- 3.1.3(b): plans bought elsewhere are honoured on iOS only if the same plans are also sold as IAP.
- 3.1.1(a): Egypt has no external-purchase-link or alternative-payment entitlement (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/skexternalpurchasemultilink.md), so the app may not steer users to other payment methods.
- 3.1.2(a)/(c) and Schedule 2: one subscription group; clear title, length and price; Terms and Privacy links (https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/).
- 2.1(b): IAPs must be complete, visible to the reviewer and functional.
